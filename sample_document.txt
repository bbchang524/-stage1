AI Study Assistant is a prototype that helps students review English course materials. Students often spend substantial time locating important passages in long readings. The application allows users to upload a text-based PDF or TXT document. It identifies representative sentences using a frequency-based extraction method. Users can also type a question and retrieve related sentences using TF-IDF similarity. The current implementation operates locally and does not require an external API. The baseline does not generate new answers and does not use a large language model. In future work, a local language model could provide fluent answers with citations. A future deployment could explore Intel Arc Pro B70 hardware for accelerated inference, subject to actual compatibility testing. The current demo has not been benchmarked on ASUS hardware. Its main value is a simple and transparent document navigation workflow.
